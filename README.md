College**, and built out as open, hands-on repositories students actually run.

## Research

Low-resource NLP for Romanized Nepali and code-mixed text. My paper
*"NLP-Driven Chatbot for Algorithm Learning: A RAG Approach using Romanized
Nepali and English"* (Aadim Journal, 2026) shows that a code-switch-aware RAG
pipeline lifts DSA learning outcomes by 11.7pp over conventional instruction
(d = 1.17, p < .001) — implemented in full at
[meroAlgorithm](https://github.com/saroj-25/meroAlgorithm).

Alongside training, this lab does research: [meroAlgorithm](https://github.com/saroj-25/meroAlgorithm)
is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation chatbot for DSA education in Romanized Nepali
and English — a full reproduction of a published paper, with a working retrieval
pipeline, statistical evaluation, and a Streamlit demo.


## About the research

This implements my paper, *Natural Language Processing-Driven Chatbot for
Algorithm Learning: A Retrieval-Augmented Generation Approach using Romanized
Nepali and English* (Aadim Journal of Multidisciplinary Research Information &
Technology, 2(1), 132–149, 2026) — code-switch detection, hybrid dense+BM25
retrieval, and register-preserving generation for Nepali CS students who think
in Romanized Nepali but study in English-only material.
